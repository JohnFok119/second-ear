What you are seeing

  • Glowing dots: where each sound really is
  • Bright patches on the bubble: where the AI hears sound
  • Blue lines: the direction the AI picks
  • White beads: the true direction
  • The little figure in the chair is the listener (nose = facing)
  • Floor bars: how loud the room is at each spot, right now
  • Click the floor to clap
  • "What I hear" dims the floor on the side your working ear cannot reach
  • Switch to Left only to hear like someone deaf in the right ear
