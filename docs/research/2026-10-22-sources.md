## Human single-sided-deafness (SSD) localization — comparison anchors
- Aruffo et al. 2025, Audiology Research 15(4):85 — 24 SSD children vs 20 normal-hearing; unsigned azimuth error 22.4° (SSD) vs 3.69° (normal). Frontal speakers only. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12286218/
- Agterberg et al. 2014, Front. Neurosci. 8:188 — 19 SSD vs 15 controls; azimuth regression gains near zero for SSD with high-frequency loss. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4082092/
- Ludwig et al. 2021, Front. Psychol. 12:753339 — 18 SSD+CI; mean offset 60.6° (hearing side) / 43.5° (implant side). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8566543/
- Pastore et al. 2020, Ear Hear. 41 — tests front-back for SSD-CI; numbers NOT FOUND (paywalled). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/pmid/33136640
- Front-back confusion rate for SSD: NOT FOUND in any source.
