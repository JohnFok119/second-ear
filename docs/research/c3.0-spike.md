# C3.0 binaural route spike: measurements

Facts only. No recommendation.

## Method
- HRIR set: HUTUBS `pp1_HRIRs_measured.sofa` (SimpleFreeFieldHRIR 1.0, 440 directions, 256 taps, **44100 Hz**, resampled to **24000 Hz** with spaudiopy/resampy). CC BY 4.0. Source position is stored as SOFA spherical (azimuth deg CCW from front, elevation deg, radius m); spaudiopy `load_sofa_hrirs` reads it that way. Our convention (+CCW, 90 = left) matches; the left-louder checks below confirm it empirically.
- Stimulus: 1 s white noise, 24 kHz, `default_rng(0)`, same for both routes. Elevation 0, azimuth -180..165 step 15.
- Route A: plane wave -> FOA (ACN W,Y,Z,X, SN3D) -> Y,Z,X x sqrt(3) (N3D) -> `spaudiopy.decoder.magls_bin(hrirs, 1)` -> `sh2bin`. Note: spaudiopy 0.2.0 calls `scipy.special.sph_harm`, removed in SciPy 1.17 (installed); `secondear.binaural.hrir` installs a one-line shim to `sph_harm_y`. No dependency added. magLS default transition frequency is N*600 = 600 Hz: above it only magnitudes are matched, not phase.
- Route B: noise convolved with the angularly nearest HRIR pair (exact grid hits at these azimuths).
- ITD: STFT (repo `features/stft.py`, 1024 FFT, hop 480), `gcc_phat` left vs right (lag +-24 samples), averaged over frames, parabolic peak interpolation. Positive = left lags right. Reported broadband and with bins above 1.5 kHz zeroed ("<1.5k").
- ILD: 10 log10 of left/right energy from the whole-signal FFT (left minus right), broadband and bands <1.5 kHz, 1.5-6 kHz, >6 kHz.
- Front/back: left-ear 1/3-octave band levels (centres 1000*2^(k/3) Hz, 200 Hz-10 kHz), RMS of the band-level difference between azimuth theta and 180-theta, mean level removed (shape only). Pairs theta = -75..75 (+-90 excluded: identical).
- Code: `src/secondear/binaural/`, `scripts/spike_binaural.py`; figure `docs/research/c3.0-spike.png`.

## Observations (numbers only)
- ILD: Route A follows Route B within a few dB below 6 kHz; >6 kHz differs by 6.05 dB RMS (A up to 8-9 dB higher/lower at several azimuths, e.g. az -150: A -18.1 vs B -9.5).
- ITD: Route B peaks at about +-706 us broadband (az -90/+90; 867 us at +105). Route A <1.5 kHz peaks at +299 us (az -90) and -201 us (az +90), about 27-40 % of B. Route A broadband GCC-PHAT ITD is about +80 us for az -45..+45 (not zero at az 0), then drifts to 8 us at az +90, i.e. ~0 and wrong sign at az +90 (sanity FAIL on broadband ITD only). ITD <1.5k passes for both routes at +-90.
- Route A has a +50..+80 us offset at az 0 (B: -0.5 broadband, 2.8 <1.5k).
- Front/back mean spectral difference: A 2.05 dB, B 2.78 dB. A is lower than B for lateral-to-front pairs (e.g. 45/135: A 1.53, B 3.86; 75/105: A 0.52, B 2.73) and similar or higher for pairs near the median plane (e.g. -30/-150: A 2.86, B 1.99).
- Test: `tests/test_binaural.py` marks the Route A +90 broadband-ITD case `xfail(strict=True)` to record the finding; the other 7 cue-sanity checks pass.
